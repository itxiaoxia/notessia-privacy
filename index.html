Notessia Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 8, 2026

Notessia is a local-first Markdown note-taking application for personal writing, knowledge management, local export, local network collaboration, and device-to-device transfer. This Privacy Policy explains what information Notessia accesses, stores, transmits, and how that information is used.

Notessia does not sell personal information, does not use advertising SDKs, and does not track users across apps or websites for advertising or marketing profiling.

1. Local-First Data Storage

Notessia is designed around a local-first architecture. Your notes, folders, editor content, undo history, settings, and related app state are stored on your device unless you choose to export, share, sync, transfer, or submit information yourself.

Notessia does not operate a centralized cloud note server for storing your notes. Because your data is stored locally, you are responsible for keeping backups of important notes and files.

2. Information Notessia May Access or Store

  • Notes, folders, Markdown content, editor state, and document history stored locally on your device.
  • App preferences, language settings, license state, trial state, and local agreement state.
  • Local debug or crash information generated by the operating system or the app when needed to diagnose failures.
  • Local network identifiers, display names, sharing permissions, collaborator presence, and peer connection information when you use collaboration or local transfer features.
  • Feedback text and optional contact email address if you choose to submit feedback from inside the app.

3. Local Network Collaboration and Transfer

If you enable local network sharing, collaboration, or device transfer, selected note data and collaboration metadata may be transmitted directly between devices or collaborators that you choose to connect with on the same trusted network.

These features are initiated by you. You should only use them with trusted devices, trusted networks, and collaborators you intend to share with.

4. Feedback

If you submit feedback, Notessia may transmit the feedback message, platform information, submission time, and optional contact email address to a feedback delivery provider so that we can receive, understand, and respond to your feedback.

Feedback is optional. Please do not include passwords, payment information, private notes, confidential documents, or other sensitive information in feedback messages.

5. Purchases and License Status

Purchases, trials, license checks, and restore-purchase actions may be handled by the platform store, such as Microsoft Store or Apple Store, depending on the version you install. Notessia does not receive or store your payment card information.

The app may use store-provided license, receipt, entitlement, product, trial, or purchase status information to determine whether features are available, whether a trial is active, or whether a purchase has been restored.

6. Data Sharing

Notessia may share or transmit information only in the following cases:

  • When you choose to share, collaborate, sync, transfer, export, or submit feedback.
  • When a platform store processes purchases, trials, receipts, or license status.
  • When required to comply with applicable laws, regulations, legal process, or enforceable governmental requests.

7. Security

Notessia uses the security controls provided by your operating system and local device storage. Local network collaboration and transfer features should be used only on trusted networks. No software can guarantee absolute security, so you should keep your device, operating system, and backups protected.

8. Your Choices and Controls

  • You can create, edit, export, delete, or back up your local notes from within the app or through your device storage.
  • You can choose whether to use local network sharing, collaboration, transfer, and feedback features.
  • You can remove locally stored app data by deleting the app data from your device, subject to your operating system's behavior.
  • If you submitted feedback and want to request deletion of your feedback message or contact email, contact us using the address below.

9. Children's Privacy

Notessia is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through feedback, please contact us.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
